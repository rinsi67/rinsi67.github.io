ClipStudio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

ClipStudio runs entirely on the owner's local computer (Windows). It does not operate a server that stores personal data of third parties.

What the application handles

  • video clips recorded from the owner's own Twitch stream;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's own accounts (YouTube, TikTok);
  • captions, titles, tags and publication status of the clips.

Where the data is stored

All files and tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Tokens are kept in local files and are sent only to the official platform APIs they belong to.

TikTok

The application requests the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish. The basic profile (display name, avatar) is used only to show which account is connected. Video files are uploaded to TikTok only when the owner publishes a clip.

Sharing

No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are used. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Removal

The owner can disconnect TikTok in the application (the stored tokens are deleted) or revoke access in the TikTok account settings.
